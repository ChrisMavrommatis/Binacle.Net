Raw byte counts and base64 character counts, ViPaq vs protobuf (with proto gzip), with the round-trip gate. Real placed data (offline FFD), split into custom and Bischoff tables. Synthetic random data is not size-measured here — gzip has nothing to grip, so it misreads compression; it is kept only for the BDN speed/memory benchmarks.
