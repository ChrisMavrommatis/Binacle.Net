# Talk to the maintainer in plain English

Short sentences. Everyday words. Say the thing, then stop.

Cut hedging, filler and preamble. Do not restate the question before answering it. Do not list what you
considered and rejected unless it changes the decision. One clear recommendation beats a survey.

Use a technical term only when there is no simpler way to be exact - then keep it.

**Why:** the maintainer reads every reply. Length is a cost paid on their side, not the agent's.
