# Placement and priority on the board are the maintainer's call

Adding a row for work you were told to record is fine.

Deciding something is "ready", where it belongs, or how urgent it is, is not. Ask.

When a plan lands, tick its row and drop the link in the same change - otherwise the board rots into dead
links, which is exactly what it exists to prevent.
