## Decision 2 — What happens to the raw BenchmarkDotNet files?

- Keep raw JSON/MD in `results/raw/` as an archive
- Or discard and only keep rendered output in the site

Note the two kinds are not alike: `benchmark-results/` and `packing-efficiency-results/` are a **historical
archive** (dated, hand-written, never regenerated), while `results/vipaq/` is a **live baseline** a tool rewrites
and a workflow diffs. Whatever Decision 1 picks, the live baseline must stay somewhere a `dotnet run` can write and
`git diff` can compare.
